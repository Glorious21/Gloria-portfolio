About

I'm a backend developer based in Lagos, working across community finance, IoT, and blockchain. I've built and shipped projects at hackathons like Hack4Freedom Lagos and Suioverflow, and I'm currently part of Dada Devs Cohort 5, an open-source Bitcoin engineering program. Whether it's a savings platform, a hackathon prototype, or an open-source PR, I build with authenticity over polish-for-show
